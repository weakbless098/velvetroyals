Need to Know

Common Questions

01

Do you offer same-day delivery?

+

Yes! We take orders 24 hours a day. Place your order before 14:00 and we will deliver the same day. Orders placed after 14:00 are scheduled for the next available date, or we'll confirm same-day availability with you on WhatsApp.

02

What areas do you deliver to?

+

We deliver across all major UAE cities. Delivery fees vary by area and are clearly shown at checkout before you confirm your order.

03

How fresh are the flowers?

+

All flowers are sourced fresh from trusted growers and arranged the same day they are delivered. Most bouquets stay fresh for 7–14 days when cared for properly.

04

Can I include a personalized message?

+

Absolutely. There is a "Special Notes" field at checkout where you can write any message or instructions. We will include a printed card with your order.

05

What payment methods do you accept?

+

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), bank transfer, and online card payments via Visa/Mastercard.

06

Can I cancel or modify my order?

+

You can request a cancellation or modification by contacting us immediately after placing. Orders already prepared or out for delivery cannot be cancelled.

07

What is the Custom Order Builder?

+

It lets you mix and match: choose an arrangement style, your flower type, and optionally add a gift, then add the whole bundle to your cart in one click.
