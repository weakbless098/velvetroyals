Our Story

Born from a love of beauty and a desire to bring joy. Velvet Royals Flowershop is a premium floral studio dedicated to crafting meaningful moments, one bouquet at a time.

Who We Are

Velvet Royals Flowershop is a luxury floral studio based in the UAE, specializing in bespoke arrangements for every occasion, from intimate celebrations to grand events.

We believe flowers are more than decoration. They are expressions of love, gratitude, and connection. Every stem is handpicked, every bouquet is crafted with intention, and every delivery is made with care.

Our team of experienced florists brings artistry and passion to every order, ensuring that what arrives at your door is nothing short of extraordinary.
