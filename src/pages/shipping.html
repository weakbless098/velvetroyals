Shipping & Delivery

Last updated: June 2026

We deliver fresh flowers across the UAE with care and speed. Here is everything you need to know about our delivery service.

Delivery Areas & Fees

  • Dubai: AED 25
  • Sharjah: AED 35
  • Ajman: AED 70
  • Umm Al Quwain / Ras Al Khaimah: AED 90
  • Abu Dhabi / Al Ain: AED 150
  • Fujairah: AED 150

Same-Day Delivery

Orders placed before 14:00 qualify for same-day delivery. Orders placed after 14:00 will be scheduled for the next available delivery date.

Delivery Time Slots

  • 09:00 – 12:00 (Morning)
  • 12:00 – 15:00 (Afternoon)
  • 15:00 – 18:00 (Evening)
  • 18:00 – 21:00 (Night)

In-Store Pickup

Free in-store pickup is available during business hours. You can select a pickup date and time slot at checkout.

Delivery Confirmation

You will receive a WhatsApp message when your order is on its way. Please ensure someone is available to receive the delivery.

Failed Delivery

If a delivery attempt fails due to incorrect address or no one being available, we will contact you to arrange re-delivery. An additional delivery fee may apply.

Contact

For delivery enquiries, WhatsApp us at +971 50 744 3100.
